Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · Version française

In short: your photos and videos never leave your device. Allège has no server, no account and no ads. We collect no data.

Your photos and videos

Allège only accesses the photos and videos you pick yourself in the system picker. They are made lighter directly on your device, with the tools built into the system, without any Internet connection. They are never sent to any server, ours or anyone else's.

The lighter copies are created in a temporary folder of the app. They only leave your device when you share them yourself, to the app of your choice. “Save” adds them to your photo library (or to the folder of your choice on Mac). Your originals are never changed.

Location and metadata

Photos often contain hidden information: where they were taken, the device, the date. When the “Remove location and details” option is on (it is by default), this information is stripped from the lighter copy. Allège does not read, keep or transmit it.

Data kept on your device

Allège only keeps your last settings, the fact that you have seen the tutorial, your language choice, and the total of what you have made lighter (a number of bytes and files). This information never leaves your device.

Contacting us

From the app, the “Help and contact” menu opens this site's support page in your browser. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages: visiting it falls under GitHub's privacy policy, like any website.

If you email us, we only use your address to reply to you, and never share it.

Children

Since Allège collects no data, the whole family can use it safely.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page, with its update date.
