Frequently asked questions

Which quality should I pick for photos?

“Balanced” fits almost every case: the difference is invisible on screen and the size often drops by half or more. “Light” goes further, for quick sharing. “Faithful” keeps the most detail, for prints.

What is the maximum size for?

It limits the longest side of the image. “4K” (3840 px) stays sharp full screen and for printing, “Full HD” (1920 px) is perfect for screens and sharing, “Web” (1280 px) for a website or an email. “Original” keeps the dimensions and only reduces the weight. A photo smaller than the limit is never enlarged.

Which format should I pick?

“Auto” keeps HEIC as HEIC on iPhone and Mac, and turns everything else into JPEG, readable anywhere. PNG is lossless and keeps transparency, but it is heavier: best for screenshots and logos.

One of my photos barely changed.

It was already heavily compressed. Allège then retries with stronger compression and, if the file is still heavier, gives you back the original. If you asked to remove the GPS location, the cleaned photo is kept even if it weighs a little more.

What does “Remove location and details” do?

Photos often contain the exact place where they were taken, the device model and the date. This option strips them from the lighter photo, so you can share it without revealing where you were. Your original photos are not changed.

Which quality should I pick for a video?

“Message” (480p) fits anywhere, by message or email. “Balanced” (720p) stays sharp on a phone. “High quality” (1080p) is barely different from the original. The estimated size is shown before you start.

Compressing a video takes a while.

It depends on the length and on your device: a few seconds for a short clip, several minutes for a long film. The screen stays on during compression. The “Cancel” button stops it at any time.

Are my originals deleted?

No, never. Allège creates a lighter copy. Tap “Save” to add it to your photos (“Allège” album) or “Share” to send it. You can then delete the original yourself if you wish.

“Save” does not work.

Allège needs permission to add photos. Open Settings > Allège > Photos and choose “Add Photos Only” or “Full Access”. On Mac, “Save” asks you to choose a folder.

How do I change the language?

By default, Allège follows your device language. To change it, tap “…” at the top right of the home screen, then “Language”.

How do I replay the tutorial?

Tap “…” at the top right of the home screen, then “Replay the tutorial”.

I bought the app on iPhone, do I need to buy it again on Mac?

No. Allège is a universal purchase: with the same Apple ID, you download it for free on your Mac from the Mac App Store.

Is there a subscription or ads?

No. Allège is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription, no ads, no in-app purchase, and no account to create.
